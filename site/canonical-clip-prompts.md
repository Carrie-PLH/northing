# Canonical Baseline Clip Prompts

Six ready-to-paste Veo prompts — one static canonical-anchored clip per remaining location
(BM-001 already done). Pattern proven on Beaver Meadow: first-frame conditioning, one camera,
one length, the location's structural fact stated positively and banned negatively.

Production material, not canon. For each: attach the named canonical image in Veo's
image-to-video / first-frame mode, paste the prompt, accept 4–8 seconds.

NOTE on first-frame behavior (observed): with a reference image attached, Veo locks palette,
season, weather, and surface character to the image and ignores style/treatment language. So:
(1) the ARCHIVAL/TREATMENT section in these prompts is inert on first-frame runs — film grain
and the amateur costume are applied in post (ffmpeg), not in generation; (2) seasonal or
mood-state variants are a TWO-STEP: image-to-image edit the canonical still first ("this exact
scene, mid-winter — change nothing about landform or composition"), then animate the variant
still as the new first frame. Accepted variant stills become secondary, non-canonical reference
assets — each location's identity bundle.

- Image-to-video from the attached reference: match its aspect ratio and hold its landform

- Render FULL FRAME: no film-strip borders, sprocket holes, frame edges, timestamps, or scan
  artifacts baked into the image.
- The footage should read as a record of a place, not a polished production.

SOUND DIRECTION
- Ambient field recording only (no music, no narration).
- Layered water — one channel quicker than the other — wind in reeds, intermittent insect or
  frog, distant bird.

NEGATIVE INSTRUCTIONS
- not a single stream, not a pond, not a lake — two channels remain distinct as they meet
- no people, text, captions, logos, or watermarks
- no surreal distortion, morphing, or impossible physics
- no fantasy, supernatural, horror, or menace
- no spectacle, drama, or movie-trailer pacing
- no fast push-ins, whip pans, sweeping reveals, or drone/aerial tourism style
- no over-saturated color, HDR clarity, lens flares, or glamour lighting
- no excessive cinematic polish, AI smoothing, or plastic surface texture
- for this location specifically, avoid: over-saturated greens, glamour-light reflections,
  postcard symmetry, any sense of a manicured garden pond

FINAL INSTRUCTION TO VEO
Hold a single, quiet, physically realistic 8-second static shot of this place exactly as the
reference frames it — two watercourses meeting in a wet meadow. Let the water and the air carry
all motion. The place is self-sufficient and indifferent to the viewer. Do not add narrative,
drama, people, text, or spectacle. Observe, do not present.
