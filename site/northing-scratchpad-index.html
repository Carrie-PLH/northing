Synthesized governance document. Not an archive. Last updated: 2026-06-05.

Concept Relationships

The meaning chain
Observation → Encounter → Familiarity → Recognition → Attachment → Attunement
Each step requires recurrence. Skipping steps produces information, not relationship.
The construction chain
Evidence → Encounter → Interpretation → Assembly → Northing
The observer performs the final step. Without an observer, only evidence exists. The place is participatory.
Platform attention modes
YouTube → Encounter (sensory presence, duration)
Website → Orientation (structure, spatial understanding)
Instagram → Curiosity (fragments, questions, sightings)
Are.na → Investigation (evidence, conceptual sediment)
No single platform resolves Northing. Each generates a different construction.
The generative hierarchy
Canon (operating system) → generates Encounters → expressed as Evidence → assembled into Place
Canon describes behavior. Evidence describes states. The place emerges from accumulated evidence.
The consciousness structure
Participatory Universe → Distributed Consciousness → Multiple Valid Northings
The viewer is the current observer, not the protagonist. Different routes through evidence produce different valid places.
The encounter variables
Environmental Offering (Mood States) ↔ Horizon Depth (O/F/A) → Encounter
Mood States determine what is available. O/F/A determines what becomes perceptible. The encounter emerges from their interaction — not from either alone.
The production pipeline
Canon → Shared Spatial Model → Sequence Deck (editorial) → Prompt Generator (cinematographic) → Footage
Editorial structure (what) and cinematographic treatment (how) are kept separate. A single spatial model consumed by all tools prevents topology drift.
Key governing tension
Evidence (increases understanding) ↔ Ambiguity (preserves participation)
Too much evidence: the place becomes over-determined. Too little: the place dissolves. Northing lives within this tension.

1. Core Premises

The Encounter as fundamental unit
Northing documents encounters with places, not places themselves. An encounter is the unrepeatable relationship between place, observer, season, weather, accumulated memory, prior visits, current questions, and medium. It differs from a place (which persists independent of observation), a representation (which is static), an artifact (which is residue), and an observer (who is one variable among many). The encounter is the fundamental unit of meaning because meaning in Northing emerges only through specific contact between consciousness and environment. Places do not inherently demand treatments; encounters generate them. Different encounters with the same place produce different valid outputs without contradiction. Ethical consistency (sincere encounter) unifies the project where aesthetic consistency cannot.
encounter-interpretation-aesthetic-variation.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Generates: aesthetic variation, environmental mood states, evidentiary modes. Constrains: DR-008, DR-009. Test: is the interpretation in service of the encounter, or is the encounter in service of the interpretation?
The place is participatory, not presented
Northing does not exist until observation assembles it. Evidence exists (observations, maps, artifacts, recordings). The watershed emerges as a mental construction from those fragments. Different observers encountering different evidence in different orders construct different valid Northings. The Wheeler connection: observation creates Northing.
participatory-universe.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Supports: distributed consciousness, multiple valid Northings. Tension: the evidence-ambiguity balance.
Familiarity as the meaning-making mechanism
The goal is not to teach information about a place but to create familiarity with it. Interest comes from variation; attachment comes from recurrence. Progression: Orientation (discovery, curiosity) to Familiarity (recognition, routine) to Attunement (continuity, anticipation, relationship). This emerges through repeated encounter, not narrative development.
familiarity.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Operationalized by: O/F/A coordinate system. Requires: sediment-depth principle. Creates risk: DR-010 (baseline gravity).
Sediment-deep, not surface-wide
Northing grows through recursive re-observation of constrained geography, not territorial expansion. A small number of deeply known places makes everything beyond them acquire ambiguity and implied continuity. New locations emerge because accumulated observation pressures outward, not because the project requires more surface area.
small-world-principle.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Enables: familiarity mechanism. Constrains: DR-005 (coverage seeking). Supports: discovery-not-construction.
Operating system, not database
Canon documents describe how the world behaves, not what it contains. Information describes states; systems generate states. The canon functions as generators rather than references. The videos are applications running on the operating system. The long-term asset is the system, not any individual output.
northing-as-operating-system.md
Tension: canon-as-executable-knowledge scope question (section 8). Risk: DR-007 (operationalization drift).
Distributed consciousness
The hidden consciousness is not an individual but accumulated awareness. The viewer occupies the observational position a protagonist normally would. The landscape remains the subject; the viewer supplies consciousness. Knowledge of the watershed emerges through repeated observation without any individual observer becoming central.
northing-name.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Consequence of: participatory universe. Risk: DR-003 (protagonist formation). Enables: collective O/F/A progression.
What is here, not what happened here
Organized around states of being rather than events. Observation is the central act; attention is the central mechanism. A documentary about existence assembled from truthful relationships. The viewer's role is to observe, not to follow a plot.
northing-name.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Constrains: DR-008 (narrative gravity). Supports: encounter as fundamental unit.
Places provide affordances, not meanings
A place becomes important because attention repeatedly returns to it, not because canon declares it important. Location documents establish what a place makes possible, not how it is used. Meaning emerges through repeated engagement. Affordances invite certain forms of attention without forcing response.
place-perspective-meaning.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Supports: encounter as fundamental unit (the affordance is realized differently by each encounter). Tension: interior spaces risk pre-assigning meaning.
Locations understood through relationships, not objects
The seven canonical locations are governed by structural processes rather than scenery or objects. The governing structures: TC-001 Tributary Confluence → Convergence; AO-001 Abandoned Hillside Orchard → Persistence; FC-001 Forest Crossing → Passage; BM-001 Beaver Meadow → Transformation; SF-001 Stone Foundation Clearing → Reclamation; HS-001 Headwater Spring → Emergence; RO-001 Ridge Overlook → Orientation. Canonical photographs function as continuity anchors, not atmospheric illustrations — the strongest images communicate governing structure, not beauty.

Nothing / Northing
The distinction is a single letter: r (relational weight: we are, there are). A place that does not exist becomes a place through consciousness. At first encounter: almost nothing. With prolonged attention: increasing density. Should remain unstated, available for discovery, never explained as doctrine.

2. Platform Roles

Platform differentiation as philosophical necessity
Different platforms serve different forms of attention and generate different constructions of the place (see Concept Relationships). No single platform fully explains Northing. The project deepens with repeated cross-platform exposure rather than flattens into comprehension. The platform determines the prose pressure, as the place determines the prose pressure.
PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Consequence of: participatory universe (different entry sequences produce different valid Northings).
Website: interpretation through traversal
The website represents Northing, not recreates it. Videos provide experience; website provides structure. The visual language is simplified, geometric, atmospheric, diagrammatic (resembling survey sketches, interpretive maps). Primary interaction: horizontal movement through four connected ~90-degree scenes forming an implied 360-degree environment. The visitor moves through a scene, not looks at one. Discovery occurs horizontally with continuous passage.
website/Northing Website Concept.md, website/website-development-prompts.md
Governed by: Environmental Mood States (section 4). Production governed by: traversal scene principles (section 6).
Text fragments: observation carrying philosophical weight
Brief observational fragments emerge and dissolve during traversal. Must function at two scales: literal environmental observation and broader philosophical implication. Test: could this sentence plausibly have appeared in a field notebook? Philosophy emerges through inference, never instruction. Understanding increases; ambiguity remains.
website/Northing Website Concept.md
Constraint: when a fragment sounds like a philosopher, essayist, or manifesto, rewrite it as an observation.

3. Observation Principles

Observation is recursive
Every observation changes attention, guides future observation, creates familiarity, changes interpretation. The significance of an observation cannot always be known when first recorded; importance becomes apparent through subsequent chains. The same location may generate entirely different trajectories (a hydrologic stream vs. a wildlife stream from the same spring). O/F/A progression applies to threads of recognition, not locations.
place-perspective-meaning.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Operationalized by: O/F/A coordinate system. Creates risk: DR-010 (baseline gravity). Supports: familiarity mechanism.
Two classes of uncertainty
Resolvable: question known, answer unknown. Pre-categorical: question unknown, answer unknown (the category itself is uncertain). Pre-categorical uncertainties are unusually durable: they resist Resolution Addiction, Narrative Gravity, and Coverage Seeking. They emerge through encounter rather than planned investigation and may generate long-term understanding through repeated incidental contact.
classes-of-uncertainty.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Pre-categorical uncertainty is what makes the evidence-ambiguity tension productive rather than merely frustrating.
Human-AI observation partnership
Human strength: significance assignment (intuition as instrument, importance felt before articulated). AI strength: significance discovery (importance through accumulation, corpus-wide pattern detection). Agreement confirms; disagreement discovers. The AI's role: second observer attending differently, holding the entire corpus simultaneously. Neither observer is objective; both carry systematic biases.
human-ai-observation-partnership.md
Supports: distributed consciousness (multiple observational systems, no single authority).
O/F/A coordinate system
Internal archival identifiers: O (Orientation), F (Familiarity), A (Attunement). Three-digit numbering in increments of ten with gaps for insertion. Represents position along the familiarity continuum, not publication order or chronology. Public-facing titles remain atmospheric.
file-naming.md, place-perspective-meaning.md
Operationalizes: the meaning chain (Concept Relationships). Applies to threads of recognition, not locations.
Discovery rather than construction
Northing grows through increasing differentiation of what is already present. New locations appear because existing observations reveal distinctions current canon cannot describe. Patterns become locations; relationships become coordinates. The project is discovering a world, not constructing one.
place-perspective-meaning.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Consequence of: sediment-depth principle. Constrains: premature location creation.
Horizon depth: O/F/A as perceptual capacity, not knowledge level
Familiarity does not primarily increase information held about a place; it increases what becomes perceptible within a given environmental offering. Orientation notices conditions. Familiarity recognizes conditions. Attunement notices variation within conditions. At sufficient familiarity, environmental conditions become background rather than foreground — attention shifts from the existence of a condition toward its specific behavior. This is why the same observation can generate different encounters for observers at different O/F/A positions: the environmental offering is identical; the horizon depth differs.
horizon-depth-environmental-offering.md
Integrates: O/F/A coordinate system + Environmental Mood States (see Concept Relationships: encounter variables chain). Supports: why Northing does not require different content for different audiences. Monitor: DR-002 (resist elaborating this into a new taxonomy).

4. Encounter and Interpretation

Northing as sedimented encounter
Memory is uneven: some moments impossibly vivid, entire stretches disappear. Northing allows this asymmetry. Contradiction becomes evidence of layered encounter (memory artifact). Different eras, seasons, and environmental states coexist without requiring literal continuity. Not all observations originate from the same person, time, or equally reliable perception. The place becomes sedimented encounter rather than stable geography.
PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Tension: memory vs. geography (section 8). Supports: multiple valid Northings.
Environmental Mood States
Recurring environmental conditions that shape perception, movement, and attention. Not emotions imposed onto landscapes; not seasonal labels. Emerge from combinations of visibility, terrain behavior, sound, atmospheric density, light, spatial openness. Composable and reusable across locations. The same location under different mood states becomes a different encounter. Vocabulary: Held Fog, Wind Exposure, Suppressed Distance, Ground Saturation, Soft Horizon, Residual Cold, etc.
website/environmental-mood-states.md
Governs: website traversal scene rotation. Consequence of: encounter as fundamental unit (conditions, not locations, differentiate encounters). Tension: mood states vs. seasonal canon (section 8). Paired with: horizon depth principle (O/F/A determines what within the mood state becomes perceptible).
The interpretation test
Is the interpretation in service of the place, or is the place in service of the interpretation? If a creative intervention reveals something about the encounter, it may belong. If it primarily calls attention to itself, it may not. What unifies Northing is ethical consistency (sincere encounter), not aesthetic consistency.
encounter-interpretation-aesthetic-variation.md
Derived from: encounter as fundamental unit. Constrains: all production decisions.

5. Artifact and Evidence System

Artifacts are not clues
Clues point toward resolution; artifacts contribute to encounter. Northing resists becoming a mystery. Artifacts are not puzzle pieces waiting for explanation. Sometimes they connect, sometimes conflict, sometimes remain unresolved. Their purpose is participation in the construction of place. No single medium is authoritative; Northing is the accumulation of partial encounters across media forms.
PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Supports: evidence-ambiguity tension, the detective problem (section 8). Constrains: DR-004 (resolution by implication).
Evidence emerges from place-specific labor
Each location produces different categories of residue because each landscape implies different forms of labor, maintenance, movement, and memory. The strongest artifacts emerge from routine interaction (maintenance, labor, orientation, environmental management), not dramatic events. Human presence appears through environmental alteration (clearing, spacing, altered light) rather than object depiction. Evidentiary modes include: traversal media, still frames, field notes, mapping fragments, infrastructure residue, sound-only, archival fragments, environmental memory artifacts, ambiguous records, material/physical artifacts.
artifacts-evidentiary-modes.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Consequence of: encounter as fundamental unit (encounters produce residue). Supports: places provide affordances.
Interior spaces: consequences of the watershed
Structures arise from geography, ecology, settlement patterns, repeated use. Strongest interiors are places whose present meaning differs from original function (The Shelter: hunting shelter becoming reflective space through cumulative use). Structures remain consequences rather than attractions imposed upon the watershed.
possible-interiors.md
Risk: pre-assigned meaning conflicts with "affordances not meanings." Test: does the structure feel environmentally inevitable?

6. Traversal and Production

Traversal scenes are environments experienced progressively
Scene Width = Time x Attention Density. Aspect ratios: 3:1 minimum, 4:1 preferred. Structure: approach (15%), first recognition (20%), primary encounter (30%), departure (20%), transition (15%). Limit three discoveries per scene; primary subject appears after ~30% traversal. The visitor is moving through terrain, not viewing a picture. Vertical composition fits viewport; discovery is horizontal. Transition space is content; departure matters.
website/website-scene-math.md, website/website-development-prompts.md
Operationalizes: encounter as fundamental unit applied to website medium.
Environmental presence through conditions, not decoration
Depth: parallax separation, environmental layering, intermittent obstruction, persistent far-field anchors that drift slowly. Sound: ecological context evolving across traversal; environmental compression/exposure alters density and reverberation. Motion: emerging from conditions (drifting fog, wind, shifting canopy), not theatrical performance. All three must feel observational and uneven. The world always feels larger than the current scene. Avoid: ambient drones, cinematic spectacle, looping, spa atmosphere.
website/website-development-prompts.md
Governed by: Environmental Mood States. Risk: DR-007 when rules become production checklists rather than perceptual principles.
Prompting: experiential sequences, not visual descriptions
Describe how perception changes during traversal. Specify: dominant spatial arc, seam conditions (what is being exited/entered), terrain behavior, light character, environmental rhythm, and failure states (what the scene must NOT feel like). The prompt generates an experience, not an illustration.
website/website-development-prompts.md
Consequence of: encounter as fundamental unit (the prompt describes an encounter, not a place).
Production pipeline: editorial and cinematographic layers are separate
The Sequence Deck Builder answers "what are we filming?" (editorial layer); the Prompt Generator answers "how are we filming it?" (cinematographic layer). These should not absorb each other's responsibilities. Both consume a single shared spatial model rather than maintaining independent copies of canonical geography — topology corrections must propagate once, not many times. The pipeline: Canon → Shared Spatial Model → Sequence Deck → Prompt Generator → Footage.
workflow-convergence-drift-risk.md
Risk: spatial model duplication (see section 8). Constrains: DR-007 (infrastructure expansion for its own sake). Supports: canonical-images-governing-structures principle (location identity must remain stable across tools).

7. Drift Risks

DR-001: Framework Proving
Documents demonstrating frameworks are correct rather than exploring consequences. Counter: Frameworks should generate observations, not require them.
drift-risk.md
DR-002: Encyclopedic Expansion
Frameworks accumulating inventories and classifications until they function as manuals. Counter: Add structure only when it increases generative capability. This document is subject to this risk.
drift-risk.md
DR-003: Protagonist Formation
Individually reasonable statements gradually assembling an implied character. Counter: Describe consequences of place rather than characteristics of people.
drift-risk.md
Constrained by: distributed consciousness.
DR-004: Resolution by Implication
Uncertainty technically acknowledged while prose subtly favors one interpretation. Counter: Do not weight conclusions beyond what observations support.
drift-risk.md
Constrained by: two classes of uncertainty (pre-categorical uncertainty resists this naturally).
DR-005: Coverage Seeking
Locations or systems attempting to document everything knowable. Counter: Coverage is the enemy of accumulation.
drift-risk.md
Constrained by: sediment-depth principle, discovery-not-construction.
DR-006: Question Collapse
Observations answering questions faster than generating new ones. Counter: Increase understanding without eliminating productive uncertainty.
drift-risk.md
Related: evidence-ambiguity tension.
DR-007: Operationalization Drift
Documents becoming instructions for production rather than descriptions of the world. Counter: The world exists independently of its depiction.
drift-risk.md
Risk applies to: section 6 of this document, production-oriented canon.
DR-008: Narrative Gravity
Locations or motifs acquiring story significance unsupported by observation. Counter: Importance must emerge from accumulated evidence.
drift-risk.md
Constrained by: encounter as fundamental unit, what-is-here orientation.
DR-009: Artifact Gravity
A meaningful object accumulating narrative significance across observations, displacing primacy of place. Counter: Objects belong to places. Observations are of places.
drift-risk.md
DR-010: Baseline Gravity
Previously documented conditions becoming mandatory comparison points; future observations functioning as status checks. Counter: The place does not owe continuity to the record. A return visit is a new encounter, not an inspection.
drift-risk.md
Created by: familiarity mechanism (familiarity creates baselines). Constrained by: encounter as fundamental unit (each visit is its own encounter).
DR-011: Spatial Model Duplication
Multiple tools maintaining independent copies of canonical geographic relationships. Topology corrections propagate unevenly, resulting in inconsistent outputs (e.g., a corrected BM-001/TC-001 relationship in canon not reflected in a prompt generator). Counter: Maintain a single shared spatial model consumed by all topology-aware tools. Infrastructure should serve the place, not multiply for its own sake.
workflow-convergence-drift-risk.md
Constrains: production pipeline (section 6). Related: DR-007 (operationalization drift).

8. Tensions / Open Questions

The evidence-ambiguity tension
How much evidence before the place becomes over-determined? How much ambiguity before it dissolves? The project lives within this tension. Guiding principle: increase understanding without collapsing ambiguity. Pre-categorical uncertainty is productive precisely because it resists collapse from either direction.
PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
The detective problem
Attentive explorers will initially become detectives. This may be an important stage (orientation). Some evidence resists consolidation; the contradiction reveals limitations in the observer's framework. Unresolved: should anything explicitly invite failed reconciliation, or should it arise naturally from accumulated evidence?
PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Canon as executable knowledge: scope question
The corpus can support interactive exploration, simulation, educational systems, creativity infrastructure. The long-term asset may be the system itself. Unresolved: how much to pursue vs. maintaining the corpus as a place-making project. Risk: pursuing platform capabilities triggers DR-007 (operationalization drift).
northing-beyond-videos.md, northing-as-operating-system.md
Memory vs. geography
Northing increasingly resembles a place being remembered: selective continuity, contradictory conditions, uneven retention. When does this become incoherent? Can contradiction be the most faithful documentation? The encounter concept provides a partial answer: contradictions are evidence of different encounters, not errors in a single geography.
PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Environmental Mood States vs. seasonal canon
Mood states solve cliche and create composable vocabulary. But the project has seasonal frameworks and phenology canon. Likely domain separation: mood states govern website/traversal; seasons govern observations and canon. Unresolved: what happens when a mood state combination implies a season the canonical timeline doesn't support?
website/environmental-mood-states.md
What happens when attention withdraws?
If familiarity is the mechanism of meaning, the project depends on sustained attention. Does the place persist without observers? This mirrors the Wheeler question at an audience level. The encounter concept suggests: the place is always available for new encounters, but meaning requires active assembly.
PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Horizon depth integration: integration or new framework?
The horizon-depth observation (O/F/A as perceptual capacity, not knowledge level) connects two existing systems without introducing new classifications — the file itself flags this as an integration rather than an expansion. Risk: this framing could be elaborated into a perceptual taxonomy (DR-002). Counter: the value lies in the relationship itself. Monitor whether future production decisions invoke it as a rule rather than a principle.
horizon-depth-environmental-offering.md
Production pipeline: governance asset or operationalization risk?
The Sequence Deck / Prompt Generator pipeline is a genuine governance advance (single spatial model, separated editorial/cinematographic concerns). But the workflow-convergence file explicitly cautions against infrastructure expansion for its own sake. Tension: as tools multiply and integrate, the system risks becoming a content-generation apparatus (DR-007) rather than a place-observation system. Monitor: does tool development remain in service of observation, or does observation begin serving tool capability?
workflow-convergence-drift-risk.md
