Northing Scratchpad Index

Synthesized governance reference. Not an archive. Last updated: 2026-05-30.

1. Core Premises

The place is participatory, not presented
Northing does not exist until observation assembles it. Evidence exists (observations, maps, artifacts, recordings). The watershed emerges as a mental construction from those fragments. Different observers encountering different evidence in different orders construct different valid Northings. The Wheeler connection: observation creates Northing.
participatory-universe.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Sediment-deep, not surface-wide
Northing grows through recursive re-observation of constrained geography, not territorial expansion. A small number of deeply known places makes everything beyond them acquire ambiguity and implied continuity. New locations emerge because accumulated observation pressures outward, not because the project requires more surface area.
small-world-principle.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Operating system, not database
Canon documents describe how the world behaves, not what it contains. A map is information; a geographic framework is a system. Information describes states; systems generate states. The canon functions as generators rather than references. The videos are applications running on the operating system.
northing-as-operating-system.md
Familiarity as the meaning-making mechanism
The goal is not to teach information about a place but to create familiarity with it. Interest comes from variation; attachment comes from recurrence. Progression: Orientation (discovery, curiosity) to Familiarity (recognition, routine) to Attunement (continuity, anticipation, relationship). This progression emerges through repeated encounter, not narrative development.
familiarity.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Nothing / Northing
The distinction is a single letter: r. "R" carries relational weight (we are, there are). A place that does not exist becomes a place through consciousness. At first encounter: almost nothing. With prolonged attention: increasing density. This should remain unstated and available for discovery, never explained as doctrine.
northing-name.md
What is here, not what happened here
Northing is organized around states of being rather than events. Observation is the central act; attention is the central mechanism. A documentary about existence assembled from truthful relationships. The viewer's role is to observe, not to follow a plot.
northing-name.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Places provide affordances, not meanings
A place becomes important because attention repeatedly returns to it, not because canon declares it important. Location documents establish what a place makes possible (affordances), not how it is used. Meaning emerges later through repeated engagement.
place-perspective-meaning.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Distributed consciousness
The hidden consciousness is not an individual but accumulated awareness. The viewer occupies the observational position a protagonist normally would. The landscape remains the subject; the viewer supplies consciousness. If you are viewing Northing, you are the current participant in a larger continuum of observation.
northing-name.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md

2. Platform Roles

Platform differentiation as philosophical necessity
Different platforms serve different forms of attention. YouTube: sensory encounter / the territory. Instagram: questions / sightings / memory fragments. Are.na: evidence / conceptual sediment / underlying geology. Website: representation of accumulated understanding / the map. No single platform fully explains Northing. The project deepens with repeated exposure rather than flattens into comprehension.
PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Website as interpretation, not recreation
The website should represent Northing, not recreate it. Videos provide experience; website provides understanding. Videos provide perception; website provides structure. The website is a constructed understanding derived from accumulated observation. Visitors enter a representation of how Northing is understood, not Northing itself.
website/Northing Website Concept.md, website/website-development-prompts.md
Traversal as primary interaction
The website functions as movement through a represented landscape, not browsing pages. Four connected ~90-degree scenes forming an implied 360-degree environment. The visitor is moving through a scene, not looking at one. Discovery occurs horizontally. Continuous movement; no cuts, page boundaries, or menu transitions.
website/Northing Website Concept.md, website/website-scene-math.md
Text fragments: observation carrying philosophical weight
Text appears as brief observational fragments that emerge and dissolve. Must function at two scales: literal environmental observation and broader philosophical implication. Test: could this sentence plausibly have appeared in a field notebook? The philosophy emerges through inference, never instruction. Understanding should increase; ambiguity should remain.
website/Northing Website Concept.md

3. Observation Principles

Observation changes future observation
Every observation influences what becomes noticeable next. The process is recursive: observation changes attention, guides future observation, creates familiarity, changes interpretation, guides future observation again. The significance of an observation cannot always be known when first recorded.
place-perspective-meaning.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Two classes of uncertainty
Resolvable: question known, answer unknown. Future observation may answer directly. Pre-categorical: question unknown, answer unknown. The category itself is uncertain. Pre-categorical uncertainties are unusually durable: they resist Resolution Addiction, Narrative Gravity, and Coverage Seeking. They emerge through encounter rather than planned investigation.
classes-of-uncertainty.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Human-AI observation partnership
Human strength: significance assignment (intuition as instrument). AI strength: significance discovery (importance through accumulation). Agreement confirms; disagreement discovers. The purpose is not to determine which observer is correct but to discover what neither would have seen alone. The AI's most interesting role: second observer attending differently, holding the entire corpus simultaneously.
human-ai-observation-partnership.md
O/F/A familiarity coordinate system
Internal archival identifiers: O (Orientation), F (Familiarity), A (Attunement). Three-digit numbering in increments of ten with gaps for insertion. Coordinates represent position along the familiarity continuum, not publication order or chronology. Public-facing titles remain atmospheric. The system applies to threads of recognition, not locations themselves.
file-naming.md, place-perspective-meaning.md
Multiple streams from a single location
The same location may generate entirely different trajectories of understanding. A hydrologic stream and a wildlife stream can originate at the same spring; neither is more correct. Each represents a different history of attention. O/F/A progression applies to threads of recognition rather than locations.
place-perspective-meaning.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Discovery rather than construction
Northing grows through increasing differentiation of what is already present, not territorial expansion. New locations appear because existing observations reveal distinctions current canon cannot describe. Patterns become locations. Relationships become coordinates. The project is discovering a world, not constructing one.
place-perspective-meaning.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md

4. Encounter and Interpretation

Documents record encounters, not places
Every visit emerges from a relationship between the place, the visitor, the season, the weather, accumulated memory, prior visits, current questions, and the medium of observation. No single encounter becomes definitive. The place remains larger than any representation.
encounter-interpretation-aesthetic-variation.md
Aesthetic variation is evidence of different encounters
A location does not possess a single correct aesthetic. One visit may feel quiet, another rhythmic, another archival. The place does not dictate the aesthetic; the encounter generates it. What unifies the project is not aesthetic consistency but ethical consistency: this is a sincere encounter with a place.
encounter-interpretation-aesthetic-variation.md
The interpretation test
The useful question is not whether artistic interpretation is allowed, but: Is the interpretation in service of the place, or is the place in service of the interpretation? If a creative intervention helps reveal something about the encounter, it may belong. If it primarily calls attention to itself, it may not.
encounter-interpretation-aesthetic-variation.md
Northing as a place being remembered
Memory is uneven: some moments impossibly vivid, entire stretches disappear. Northing may allow this same asymmetry. Contradiction becomes evidence of layered encounter (memory artifact). Different eras, seasons, and environmental states coexist emotionally without requiring literal continuity. The place becomes sedimented encounter.
PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Environmental Mood States (not seasons)
Literal seasonality introduces culturally preloaded associations. Instead: recurring environmental conditions that shape perception, movement, and attention. Mood states emerge from combinations of visibility, terrain behavior, sound, atmospheric density, light, spatial openness. They are experiential and perceptual, not narrative or symbolic. Composable and reusable across locations.
website/environmental-mood-states.md

5. Artifact and Evidence System

Artifacts are not clues
Clues point toward resolution; artifacts contribute to encounter. Northing should resist becoming a mystery. Artifacts are not puzzle pieces waiting for final explanation. Sometimes they connect, sometimes conflict, sometimes remain unresolved. Their purpose is participation in the construction of place.
PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Evidentiary modes (not content formats)
Categories: traversal/environmental continuity, still environmental frames, field notes/observational records, mapping/orientation artifacts, infrastructure residue, sound-only artifacts, archival/historical fragments, environmental memory artifacts, transitional/ambiguous media, material/physical artifacts. No single medium is authoritative. Northing becomes the accumulation of partial encounters across media forms.
artifacts-evidentiary-modes.md
Human presence through maintenance, not drama
The strongest artifacts emerge from maintenance, labor, orientation, and environmental management. Not dramatic events. The world becomes believable because people interacted with it routinely. Human intervention is strongest when it appears through environmental alteration (clearing, spacing, altered light) rather than object depiction.
artifacts-evidentiary-modes.md, PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Location-specific residue categories
Each location produces different categories of residue because each landscape implies different forms of labor, maintenance, movement, memory, and environmental negotiation. The orchard generates harvest tallies, spray schedules, pruning notes; the utility corridor generates vegetation management maps, tower numbering, maintenance logs.
artifacts-evidentiary-modes.md
Interior spaces: consequences of the watershed
Structures arise from geography, ecology, settlement patterns, repeated use. Strongest interiors are places whose present meaning differs from original function. The Shelter (Ridge Overlook): hunting shelter becoming reflective space through cumulative use. Spring House, Orchard Tool House, Foundation Ruin as priority candidates. Structures remain consequences rather than attractions.
possible-interiors.md

6. Video System

Traversal scenes: environments, not illustrations
Traversal scenes are discovered progressively, not viewed simultaneously. Scene Width = Time x Attention Density. Aspect ratios: 3:1 minimum, 4:1 preferred. Structure: approach (15%), first recognition (20%), primary encounter (30%), departure (20%), transition (15%). Limit to three meaningful discoveries per scene. The primary subject appears after ~30% traversal.
website/website-scene-math.md
Spatial depth requirements
All traversal scenes must maintain strong depth hierarchy. Foreground interruption, midground traversal space, persistent far-field anchors, layered atmospheric separation. Depth emerges through parallax, environmental layering, fog/haze, intermittent obstruction, scale contrast. Far-field anchors persist through movement, drift slowly, occasionally disappear. The world always feels larger than the current scene.
website/website-development-prompts.md
Ecological sound, not ambient performance
Sound reflects environmental conditions and ecological context. Avoid generic ambient drones. Audio evolves across traversal; environmental compression/exposure alters sound density, reverberation, distance perception. Avoid: soundtrack feeling, obvious looping, exaggerated wildlife, horror ambience, meditative spa atmosphere. Goal: ecological presence.
website/website-development-prompts.md
Environmental motion from conditions, not decoration
Subtle motion emerging from conditions: drifting fog, wind in grasses, shifting canopy density, precipitation. Motion alters visibility, affects perception timing, changes depth relationships. Avoid theatrical weather, dramatic storms, cinematic spectacle, constant looping. Motion should feel observational and uneven.
website/website-development-prompts.md
Prompting principle: experiential sequences, not visual descriptions
Successful prompts describe how perception changes during traversal, not aesthetic decoration. Specify: sensory progression, spatial change, terrain behavior, light character, environmental rhythm, seam conditions, and failure states (what the scene must NOT feel like). The prompt generates an experience, not an illustration.
website/website-development-prompts.md

7. Drift Risks

DR-001: Framework Proving
Documents demonstrating existing frameworks are correct rather than exploring consequences. Counter: Frameworks should generate observations, not require them.
drift-risk.md
DR-002: Encyclopedic Expansion
Frameworks accumulating inventories and classifications until they function as manuals. Counter: Add structure only when it increases generative capability.
drift-risk.md
DR-003: Protagonist Formation by Accumulation
Individually reasonable statements gradually assembling an implied character. Counter: Describe consequences of place rather than characteristics of people.
drift-risk.md
DR-004: Resolution by Implication
Uncertainty technically acknowledged while prose subtly favors one interpretation. Counter: Do not weight conclusions beyond what observations support.
drift-risk.md
DR-005: Coverage Seeking
Locations or systems attempting to document everything knowable. Counter: Coverage is the enemy of accumulation.
drift-risk.md
DR-006: Question Collapse
Observations answering questions faster than generating new ones. Counter: Increase understanding without eliminating productive uncertainty.
drift-risk.md
DR-007: Operationalization Drift
Documents becoming instructions for production rather than descriptions of the world. Counter: The world exists independently of its depiction.
drift-risk.md
DR-008: Narrative Gravity
Locations or motifs acquiring story significance unsupported by observation. Counter: Importance must emerge from accumulated evidence.
drift-risk.md
DR-009: Artifact Gravity
A meaningful object accumulating narrative significance across observations, displacing primacy of place. Counter: Objects belong to places. Observations are of places.
drift-risk.md
DR-010: Baseline Gravity
Previously documented conditions becoming mandatory comparison points; future observations functioning as status checks rather than encounters. Counter: The place does not owe continuity to the record. A return visit is a new encounter, not an inspection.
drift-risk.md

8. Tensions / Open Questions

The evidence-ambiguity tension
How much evidence can be provided before the place becomes over-determined? How much ambiguity can remain before the place dissolves? The project may live within this tension rather than resolve it. Guiding principle: increase understanding without collapsing ambiguity.
PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
The detective problem
Attentive explorers will initially become detectives seeking chronology, reconciliation, explanation. This instinct should not necessarily be resisted; it may be an important stage. But some evidence may resist consolidation. The contradiction itself becomes instructional: it reveals limitations in the observer's framework.
PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Canon as executable knowledge: scope question
The corpus can potentially support interactive exploration, simulation, conversational place intelligence, educational systems, personalized experiences, and creativity infrastructure. The long-term asset may be the system itself, not any individual output. Unresolved: how much of this to pursue vs. maintaining the corpus as a place-making project rather than a platform.
northing-beyond-videos.md, northing-as-operating-system.md
Memory vs. geography: what kind of place is Northing?
A fully seamless environment implies stable geography and a fully knowable structure. But Northing increasingly resembles a place being remembered: selective continuity, uneven retention, overlapping impressions, contradictory conditions. When does this become incoherent? Can contradiction be the most faithful documentation?
PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md
Environmental Mood States vs. seasonal structure
Mood states solve the cliche problem (autumn = nostalgia) and create composable vocabulary. But the project also has seasonal frameworks and phenology canon. Tension: how much literal seasonality to preserve vs. how fully to adopt the perceptual-condition model for website/traversal use. Likely answer: mood states govern website; seasons govern observations and canon.
website/environmental-mood-states.md
What happens when a viewer stops returning?
If familiarity is the mechanism of meaning, the project depends on sustained attention. Unlike narrative works that resolve, Northing may lose participants gradually. Does the place persist without observers? This mirrors the Wheeler question at an audience level.
PHILOSOPHICAL-THREADS.md

9. Recently Added

Environmental Mood States framework (2026-05-27)
Emerging perceptual vocabulary for website traversal: Held Fog, Wind Exposure, Suppressed Distance, Ground Saturation, Soft Horizon, Residual Cold, etc. States combine to produce distinct perceptual environments. Three candidate combinations defined with representative scenes. Rotation model: ~12 scenes, four displayed at a time, periodically rotated.
website/environmental-mood-states.md
Traversal scene mathematics (2026-05-26)
Formal composition rules for horizontal traversal: progressive discovery, approach before arrival, departure as content, transition space as content. Viewport constraint: vertical composition must fit viewport height; discovery is horizontal. New commissioning category: Traversal Scenes (environments rather than pictures).
website/website-scene-math.md
Website as representation concept (2026-05-26)
Visual language breakthrough: simplified, geometric, atmospheric, symbolic, diagrammatic artwork (resembling survey sketches, interpretive maps). The website no longer competes with videos. Four-scene 360-degree traversal structure: Forest (enclosure), Ridge (exposure), Orchard (memory), Water (continuation without closure).
website/Northing Website Concept.md
