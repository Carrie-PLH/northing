**Entry hero note:** The Ridge Overlook illustration (001) serves as the full-viewport
background on entry. The image is positioned at `center 38%` — favoring the upper
portion of the frame where the landscape, sky, and shelter sit.

The site works completely without these images. When images are present, they become
the primary experience. When they're missing, the site falls back to the typographic
layout with subtle gradient atmosphere.
